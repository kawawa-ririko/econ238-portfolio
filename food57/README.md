# FOOD-57 — Food Miles With Transport Modes

This folder is ready to publish as a simple GitHub Pages exhibit.

## Main result

For **1 tonne of food moved 1,000 km**, the illustrative 2026 factors give:

- Small truck / diesel van: **635 kg CO₂e**
- Semi truck / articulated HGV: **79 kg CO₂e**
- Rail: **26 kg CO₂e**
- Container ship: **16 kg CO₂e**
- Long-haul air freight: **899 kg CO₂e**

A striking comparison: the emissions from moving one tonne **100 km by air** are about the same as moving it **5579 km by an average container ship**.

## Source

UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ), *Greenhouse gas reporting: conversion factors 2026*:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greenhouse-gas-reporting-conversion-factors-2026

The exhibit uses the 2026 **Freighting goods** tonne-km factors. These are TTW/transport-operation factors.  
The air-freight factor used here is the long-haul/international factor **including radiative forcing (RF)**.
